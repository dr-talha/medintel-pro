Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Effective immediately

🔒

Your data belongs to you. MedIntel Pro does not sell, share, rent, or monetize any user data — ever. We have no advertising partners. We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the service.

1. Who We Are

MedIntel Pro ("we", "us", "the platform") is a free medical reference application available at app.medintel.pro. We are not a healthcare provider, insurer, or medical device manufacturer. All content is for reference and educational purposes only.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the service you requested (drug lookup, quiz, AI chat, etc.)
  • To send push notifications you explicitly enabled (drug recalls, outbreak alerts)
  • To improve data accuracy via anonymous error and feedback reports
  • To generate CME certificates where credential verification is required

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, sale to third parties, or training AI models without your explicit consent.

4. Data Security

  • Health logs: AES-256 column-level encryption. Keys stored in Supabase Vault — not accessible to our team.
  • Data in transit: TLS 1.3 enforced on all connections.
  • Uploaded files: Processed within your session and deleted within 24 hours. No long-term storage.
  • Passwords: Bcrypt hashed with minimum 12 rounds. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Access controls: Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced — your data is accessible only to your account.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / Data Subject Rights)

  • Access: Download all your data — Settings → Privacy → Export My Data
  • Deletion: Delete all health logs — Settings → Privacy → Health Logs → Delete All
  • Account deletion: Settings → Privacy → Delete Account (permanent, immediate)
  • AI chat history: Settings → Privacy → Delete Chat History
  • Portability: All exports in standard JSON format
  • Objection / restriction: Contact us at privacy@medintel.pro

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage solely to:

  • Store your authentication token (session management)
  • Cache offline drug/glossary data for faster access without internet
  • Remember your theme preference (dark/light mode)

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

MedIntel Pro fetches data from the following public, government-operated APIs. No user data is sent to these services:

  • openFDA (FDA) — drug labels and recalls
  • RxNorm / DailyMed / MedlinePlus (NLM / NIH) — drug and disease data
  • WHO GHO API / WHO Disease Outbreak News — global health data
  • disease.sh — real-time outbreak statistics
  • PubMed E-utilities — open-access research metadata

AI responses are powered by the Anthropic Claude API. Queries sent to the AI include only your question and retrieved database context — never your account details, health logs, or identity.

8. Children's Privacy

MedIntel Pro is intended for adults and healthcare professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under the age of 16. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact privacy@medintel.pro and we will delete it immediately.

9. Data Retention

  • Health logs: Retained until you delete them or delete your account
  • AI chat history: Retained until you delete it (opt-in only)
  • Uploaded files: Deleted within 24 hours of upload
  • Account data: Deleted immediately upon account deletion request
  • Anonymous analytics: Aggregated, retained for 12 months maximum

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify registered users of material changes to this policy via email at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
